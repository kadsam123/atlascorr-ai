# AtlasCorr AI — Strategy Report Templates

AtlasCorr AI supports four narrative styles:

## 1. SME-Friendly
Clear, actionable, simple.

## 2. Investor
Focused on opportunity, ROI, scalability.

## 3. Government / Trade Agency
Focused on compliance, risk, policy alignment.

## 4. Consulting / Professional
Structured, analytical, formal.

Each template includes:
- Executive summary  
- Corridor comparison  
- Risk map  
- Document checklist  
- Recommended next steps
