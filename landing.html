AI Modules

Four agents. One unified output.

Each module is a specialist. CircleBrain coordinates them into a single, coherent export strategy.

🗺️
Meridian Flow
Scores every global trade corridor on cost index, transit time, port efficiency, and political risk. Identifies bottlenecks before they hit your shipment.
Route ScoringCorridor RankingBottleneck Detection8 Live Corridors

TradeMatch
Matches your products to global markets using a multi-factor opportunity score: tariff rate, import growth, GDP trajectory, ease of trade, and category fit.
