FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a credit card to start?
No. Your 14-day free trial gives you full access to the Growth plan with no payment required.
What happens after the trial?
You choose a plan. If you don't upgrade, you keep read-only access to your existing data.
Can I change plans?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Billing is prorated automatically.
What's included in the API?
Growth and Enterprise plans include full REST API access with an API key, OpenAPI docs, and all 8 agent endpoints.
How does agent call billing work?
Included calls reset monthly. Additional calls are billed at the rates shown above and invoiced at month end.
Can I white-label CircleTrade AI?
Yes — Enterprise plan includes full white-labelling. Contact sales for a demo of the white-label version.
What is the Circle Agent Stack?
The seven AI agents that power CircleTrade AI's pipeline, now available as individual API endpoints for on-demand, deep-analysis tasks.
Do you offer refunds?
If CircleTrade AI isn't right for you within the first 30 days of a paid subscription, we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.
